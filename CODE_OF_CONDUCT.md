# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions or
any other channel — is expected to help keep it a friendly, respectful and
harassment-free place, regardless of background, experience, nationality, gender,
religion or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in your language and actions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment or discriminatory remarks.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that
do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block
contributors who repeatedly violate it.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **ismoillooff@gmail.com**. All reports will be
handled confidentially.
